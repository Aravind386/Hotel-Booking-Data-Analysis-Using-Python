
# 🏨 Hotel Booking Data Analysis using Python

### 📊 Booking Trends | Cancellation Analysis | Revenue Optimization

A data analysis project using Python to explore hotel booking patterns, reservation cancellations, pricing trends, and differences between City Hotels and Resort Hotels. The objective is to identify factors associated with cancellations and provide business insights that can help improve hotel occupancy and revenue performance.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

City Hotels and Resort Hotels face challenges related to booking cancellations, room utilisation, and revenue generation.

This project analyses hotel booking data to understand cancellation patterns, booking trends, seasonal demand, and average daily rates (ADR).

Using Python libraries such as Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn, the project explores the factors associated with cancellations and identifies opportunities to improve booking management and business performance.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyse hotel booking and cancellation rates.
- Compare booking patterns between City Hotels and Resort Hotels.
- Examine monthly booking and cancellation trends.
- Analyse average daily rates (ADR) across different periods.
- Identify patterns between room prices and cancellations.
- Explore seasonal demand and booking behaviour.
- Generate actionable recommendations to improve occupancy and revenue

## 🔍 Key Insights & Conclusions

### 1. Booking Cancellation Rate
- Approximately **37% of clients cancelled their reservations**, according to the project analysis.
- Cancellations can reduce expected room occupancy and create challenges for revenue planning.

### 2. City Hotel vs. Resort Hotel
- City Hotels receive more bookings than Resort Hotels.
- Resort Hotels may have different pricing and seasonal demand patterns.

### 3. Average Daily Rate (ADR)
- City Hotel ADR is lower than Resort Hotel ADR on some days.
- Resort Hotel rates may increase during weekends and holidays.

### 4. Monthly Booking Trends
- August is reported to have the highest number of confirmed and cancelled reservations.
- The analysis also identifies January as a month with a high cancellation count.
- Monthly patterns can help hotels plan staffing, pricing, and room availability.

### 5. Price and Cancellation Patterns
- The analysis reports more cancellations during periods with higher prices and fewer cancellations during lower-priced periods.
- This suggests a possible association between pricing and cancellation behaviour.
